# 📊 HR Analytics & Workforce Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built using Power BI. 
The goal of this dashboard is to provide HR leaders and business stakeholders with clear insights into:
* Workforce dynamic and headcount stability
* Employee turnover and separation trends
* High-risk retention tenure groups
* Managerial turnover distribution and reason analyses

It allows decision-makers to identify retention bottlenecks, optimize hiring schedules, and mitigate employee attrition.

---

## 📌 Main Features & Visuals

### 🔸 Overview & Trend Analysis
* **KPI Summary Cards:** Quick executive overview displaying Total Headcount (`214`), New Hires (`1`), Total Terminations (`6`), Voluntary Separations (`4`), and Involuntary Separations (`2`).
* **Separations vs Last Year:** Line chart comparing monthly termination counts against the previous year's baseline, highlighting peak turnover periods (e.g., April 2018).
* **Headcount Dynamics:** Trend line chart tracking monthly headcount changes vs last year to monitor net workforce growth/shrinkage.
* **New Hires Comparison:** Visual tracking of monthly onboarding performance compared to past year metrics.

### 🔸 Turnover & Demographic Deep-Dive
* **Managerial Termination Breakdown:** Bar chart displaying termination count per Manager (e.g., Amy Dunn, Elijiah Gray, Kissy Sullivan with 3 terminations each) to identify potential leadership or team-level retention issues.
* **Tenure Group & Separation Type:** Stacked bar chart classifying Voluntary vs Involuntary terminations across tenure groups (**0-36 months**, **36-60 months**, **60+ months**).
* **Headcount by Age & Experience:** Bar charts categorizing workforce distribution by Age groups (**0-35**, **35-50**, **50+**) and Experience levels (**0-36 month**, **36-60 month**, **60+ month**).
* **Separation Reason Waterfall Chart:** Detailed breakdown of employee exit reasons (e.g., unhappy, another position, attendance, performance, higher pay, career change).

### 🔸 Granular Details & Interactive Filtering
* **Employee Detail Table:** Granular matrix including Manager Name, Department, Position, Performance Score, Experience, Age, and Separation End Date.
* **Multi-Slicer Panel:** Dynamic filtering options by **Year**, **Year-Month**, **Department**, **Position**, and **Employee Name**.

---

## 🧠 Key Business Questions Answered

This dashboard answers the following strategic HR questions:
1. What is the current workforce size and how is it trending compared to the previous year?
2. At what tenure stage are employees most likely to leave voluntarily?
3. Which managers or departments experience the highest employee turnover?
4. What are the primary root causes behind employee resignations?
5. Is the recruitment pipeline keeping pace with employee exit rates?

---

## 💡 Business Value

This dashboard supports:
* **Retention Strategy Optimization:** Targeted retention programs for high-risk tenure windows (especially 3–5 year employees).
* **Managerial Coaching & Support:** Identifying teams needing leadership support or workload rebalancing.
* **Workforce Planning:** Aligning hiring cycles with attrition rates to prevent workforce depletion.
* **Data-Driven HR Policy:** Replacing intuition with objective metrics for exit reason analyses.
